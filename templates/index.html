Syllabus & DSP Theoretical Foundations

How CS30033 signal processing concepts power the assessment engine

1. 7-Step MFCC Extraction

Pre-emphasis (α=0.97) boosts high frequencies. Framed with 25ms Hamming windows (10ms hop), transformed via 512-pt FFT, mapped to 26 Mel-spaced triangular filters, logged, and decorrelated via DCT-II yielding 13 static MFCCs.

2. CMN & DTW Alignment

Cepstral Mean Normalization removes linear channel bias. Dynamic Time Warping aligns variable-speed utterances via dynamic programming, measuring minimal Euclidean trajectory distance.

3. LPC Formant Tracking

An all-pole vocal tract filter (p=16) solved via Levinson-Durbin recursion reveals resonance poles (F₁, F₂, F₃) critical for Sanskrit retroflex consonants (ष, ण).
