CS30033 Syllabus & Engineering Architecture

1. 7-Step MFCC Extraction

Speech is sampled at 16 kHz, pre-emphasized (\(\alpha = 0.97\)), framed with 25ms Hamming windows (10ms hop), transformed via 512-pt FFT to power spectrum, mapped to 26 Mel triangular filters, logged, and decorrelated via DCT-II yielding 13 static MFCCs.

2. CMN & DTW Sequence Alignment

Cepstral Mean Normalization removes channel bias and microphone gain variation. Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) aligns time-stretched phonetic sequences via dynamic programming, computing the minimum Euclidean path distance.

3. LPC Formant Tracking

All-pole model (\(p = 16\)) solved via Levinson-Durbin recursion. Deconvolves vocal tract resonance poles (formants \(F_1, F_2, F_3\)) from the glottal excitation pulse to inspect correct Sanskrit vowel articulation.
